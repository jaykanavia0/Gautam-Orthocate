## 5. Core Clinical Specialties & Surgical Spectrum

### A. Robotic Joint Replacement (Arthroplasty)
* **Robotic Total Knee Replacement (TKR):** Sub-millimeter precision implant alignment using robotic arms, reducing soft tissue trauma and accelerating post-op ambulation.
* **Partial / Unicondylar Knee Replacement (UKR):** Compartmental knee preservation for localized medial or lateral osteoarthritis.
* **Total Hip Replacement (THR):** Treatment for avascular necrosis (AVN) of the femoral head, severe osteoarthritis, and rheumatoid arthritis.
* **Shoulder Replacement:** Hemiarthroplasty and Reverse Shoulder Arthroplasty (RSA) for rotator cuff tear arthropathy and severe glenohumeral arthritis.
* **Revision Joint Surgeries:** Extraction and reconstruction of loosened or failed prior implants.

### B. Minimally Invasive Keyhole Arthroscopy & Sports Medicine
* **Knee Arthroscopy:**
  * ACL (Anterior Cruciate Ligament) Reconstruction (Hamstring / Bone-Patellar-Tendon autografts)
  * PCL (Posterior Cruciate Ligament) and multi-ligament knee repairs
  * Meniscal repairs (all-inside, inside-out suturing) & partial meniscectomy
  * Cartilage repair & microfracture techniques
* **Shoulder Arthroscopy:**
  * Rotator cuff tendon repair (supraspinatus / infraspinatus tears)
  * Bankart repair for recurrent shoulder dislocations & labral tears
  * Subacromial decompression & SLAP lesion repairs

### C. Advanced Trauma Care & Fracture Management
* High-velocity polytrauma stabilization
* Pelvic & acetabular fracture reconstruction
* Complex intra-articular fractures (tibial plateau, pilon/ankle, distal femur, distal radius)
* Minimally Invasive Plate Osteosynthesis (MIPO)
* Non-union, mal-union, and bone infection management

### D. Conservative & Regenerative Joint Preservation
* Intra-articular Viscosupplementation (Hyaluronic Acid cushioning injections)
* PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy for early chondral lesions & tendonitis
* Dedicated physical rehabilitation protocols for quadriceps (VMO), hip abductors, and hamstring balancing
* Off-loader knee bracing & biomechanical alignment correction
