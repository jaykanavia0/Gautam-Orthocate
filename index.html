CLINICAL FOCUS: KNEE DISORDERS

Knee Osteoarthritis, ACL Tears & Cartilage Wear

The knee absorbs 3 to 4 times your body weight when climbing stairs. When articular cartilage wears down or ligaments rupture, bone-on-bone friction creates severe pain and morning stiffness.

Morning stiffness lasting > 30 minutes with audible joint grinding (crepitus)
Instability or "giving way" while turning (indicative of ACL/meniscus tear)
Bowing of the legs (Varus deformity) with inability to walk without a limp

Conservative First Line

Viscosupplementation (Hyaluronic Acid injection), PRP therapy, targeted Vastus Medialis Oblique (VMO) physical therapy, and offloader bracing.

Advanced Operative Solution

Robotic Total / Partial Knee Arthroplasty for severe arthritis (24-hr post-op walking), or Keyhole Arthroscopic ACL Reconstruction using autografts.

CLINICAL FOCUS: HIP PATHOLOGIES

Avascular Necrosis (AVN) & Severe Hip Arthritis

Loss of vascular supply to the femoral head causes collapse, sharp groin pain radiating down to the thigh, and severe restriction in squatting or sitting cross-legged.

Deep groin or buttock pain worsening during walking or weight bearing
Progressive inability to tie shoes, put on socks, or sit cross-legged
Leg length discrepancy with a noticeable trendelenburg lurch/limp

Early Preservation

Core decompression for early-stage AVN, bisphosphonates, joint unloading, and specialized hip mobility protocols.

Definitive Arthroplasty

Total Hip Replacement (THR) utilizing ceramic-on-highly-crosslinked polyethylene bearings with muscle-sparing approaches for lifetime durability.

Discuss Hip Symptoms on WhatsApp
CLINICAL FOCUS: SHOULDER & SPORTS INJURIES

Rotator Cuff Tears & Recurrent Shoulder Dislocation

Shoulder instability and rotator cuff tendon ruptures prevent overhead arm movement and disturb peaceful sleep with throbbing nocturnal pain.

Unable to lift arm above head or reach behind the back without sharp agony
Shoulder joint popping out of its socket during sports or sudden motion (Bankart tear)
Night pain when sleeping on the affected side

Conservative Approach

Subacromial corticosteroid or PRP infiltration, rotator cuff conditioning, and scapular stabilization therapy.

Keyhole Arthroscopic Repair

Minimally Invasive Arthroscopic Bankart & Rotator Cuff Repair using bio-composite suture anchors through 4mm keyhole incisions.

Discuss Shoulder Care on WhatsApp
CLINICAL FOCUS: TRAUMA & COMPLEX FRACTURES

High-Velocity Skeletal Trauma & Ankle Pilon Fractures

Sudden vehicular accidents, falls, and sporting impacts cause articular fracture dislocations requiring urgent anatomical reduction to prevent joint arthritis.

Acute swelling, inability to bear weight, and visible limb deformity
Complex trimalleolar ankle fracture dislocations with joint incongruity
Old fractures failing to heal (Non-unions and Mal-unions)

Emergency Protocol

Immediate neurovascular evaluation, sterile temporary stabilization, C-arm fluoroscopy imaging, and multi-slice CT 3D mapping.

Biological Internal Fixation

Minimally Invasive Plate Osteosynthesis (MIPO) and posterolateral anatomical approach preserving soft tissue blood supply.
